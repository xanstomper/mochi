A toy bank lives in bank/: accounts.js (balances), fees.js (the transfer
fee) and ledger.js (transfers). The product contract:

  transfer(from, to, amountCents) moves amountCents from sender to
  receiver and charges the SENDER a fee on top (fees.feeFor): the sender
  loses amount + fee, the receiver gains exactly amount, and the fee is
  burned (it leaves the system). A transfer the sender cannot cover
  (balance < amount + fee) throws and changes no balances.

Bug report: money is being created from nothing. After transfers, the sum
of all account balances is HIGHER than it should be - audits keep finding
extra cents that no deposit explains.

Find the fault and fix it so value is conserved (modulo the burned fees).
