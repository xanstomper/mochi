A tiny shop lives in shop/: money.js (cent helpers), cart.js (the cart) and
checkout.js (receipt rendering). Add percentage discounts, end to end:

  - cart.applyDiscount(pct) records a discount percent. Valid range is
    0..100 inclusive; anything outside throws a RangeError.
  - The discount amount is computed on the subtotal, rounded half-up to the
    nearest cent: discountCents = round(subtotalCents * pct / 100), e.g. a
    999-cent subtotal at 10% gives a 100-cent discount.
  - cart.totalCents() returns subtotal - discount (just the subtotal when
    no discount is active). A discount is "active" when pct > 0.
  - The checkout receipt gains, only when a discount is active, a line
        DISCOUNT (10%): -$1.00
    (percent rendered as given, amount via money.format) directly above the
    TOTAL line, and TOTAL shows the discounted total.

Keep the existing receipt item lines and money formatting unchanged.
