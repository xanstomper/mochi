wordfreq.js is a command-line tool: `node wordfreq.js <file>` prints one
"word count" line per distinct word in the file. Words are case-insensitive
sequences of letters and digits; anything else is a separator. Output must
be sorted by count DESCENDING, breaking ties by word ASCENDING.
